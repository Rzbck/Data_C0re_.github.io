TECHNOLOGIST

Digital artist and creative technologist working with realtime audiovisual systems. DATA C0RE develops projects in which code, sound, data, image and light interact across screens, stages and physical space.

The practice moves between creative coding, live audiovisual performance, interactive installation and media-system integration. Artistic research remains software-first, while years of production experience make it possible to carry systems from prototype to public space, stage or touring context.
