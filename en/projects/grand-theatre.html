Projection integration

Image geometry under stage constraints.

The work covered video-system installation before performances, SMODE programming, cue creation, collaboration with the artistic team, monumental soft-edge work and the adaptation of content across strongly distorted projection planes.

Ultra-short-throw optics made small physical variations visible immediately. Final calibration therefore connected software geometry, projector placement, surface alignment and show conditions rather than treating them as separate tasks.
