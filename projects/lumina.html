Collaborative public installation / Geneva Lux / 2025—2027

LUMINA

A repeated luminous frame turns a pedestrian passage into a changing field of depth, rhythm and movement. The realtime system is inseparable from the physical architecture.

Experience

Light becomes architecture through repetition.

The work is read first as a space rather than as a screen. Nested frames exaggerate perspective and allow colour and temporal behaviour to reorganise the tunnel continuously around the visitor.
