AMEN / Church system

From simulation to architecture.

The 2016 church system moved through realtime 3D simulation, custom show control, LED / DMX / video integration and technical fabrication before becoming a live architectural environment.

3D simulation→TouchDesigner→LED / DMX / video→live architecture
02

Grenoble live systems

The same approach continued into club and stage contexts: Dave Clarke / Grenoble in 2016, realtime BPM and lighting control, then La Belle Électrique in 2018 with TouchDesigner, GLSL and Resolume. The project is presented as collaborative work rather than as a solo authorship claim.
